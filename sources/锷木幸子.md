### 东方催狐谭

<table><tbody><tr class="tt-content" id="东方催狐谭-1" data-pos="&#91;&quot;\u4e1c\u65b9\u50ac\u72d0\u8c2d&quot;,1&#93;"><td class="tt-ja" lang="ja"><div class="poem">A youkai who’s been around for a long time. The now youkai tree spirit gained the ability to seperate her body from the tree, as well as a particularly frightening ability. Yukiko could transform people into animals.</div></td><td class="tt-zh" lang="zh"><div class="poem">一只活了很久的妖怪。她平静地生活在一座茂密繁盛的森林中。<br>她过去是一株山茶树。不仅无害，每年冬天还会开出艳丽的红色花朵。<br>终于，这株山茶化作了妖怪。现在木灵能够从树上分离自己的躯体，以及另一种极其可怕的能力。她能够把人变成动物。</div></td></tr><tr class="tt-content" id="东方催狐谭-2" data-pos="&#91;&quot;\u4e1c\u65b9\u50ac\u72d0\u8c2d&quot;,2&#93;"><td class="tt-ja" lang="ja"><div class="poem">Throughout time, she started to become a pretty big thorn in Gensokyo’s side.<br>She would often seduce humans into her domain with soothing scents, relaxing auras, and sometimes just her mere presence as a beautiful maiden.<br>Her victims would be transformed into bees for her hive.<br>Rumors circulated about the mysterious, dangerous, and yet beautiful woman from the forest who would bewitch anyone that came near her.<br>When word of this reached the Hakurei Shrine Maiden (who at the time, of course, was not Reimu), they set off to exterminate this troublesome youkai.</div></td><td class="tt-zh" lang="zh"><div class="poem">随着时间的推移，她逐渐转变成了扎在幻想乡的一根毒刺。<br>她常常会诱惑人类进入自己的地盘，有时借助舒心的芬芳、有时借助轻松的气氛、有时则干脆化作一个美丽的女子。<br>她会把受害者变成自己巢穴里的蜜蜂。<br>有一位来自森林中的女子，神秘、危险而美丽，会把任何接近者迷住。这个传言不胫而走。<br>故事传到博丽神社的巫女耳中（那个时期的巫女，当然，不是灵梦），她立刻出动消灭这只麻烦的妖怪。</div></td></tr><tr class="tt-content" id="东方催狐谭-3" data-pos="&#91;&quot;\u4e1c\u65b9\u50ac\u72d0\u8c2d&quot;,3&#93;"><td class="tt-ja" lang="ja"><div class="poem">A long and grueling battle was fought, but the shrine maiden was victorious and Yukiko freed the human villagers from her treacherous curse. <br><br>So, if not humans, what are all those bees in her hive?</div></td><td class="tt-zh" lang="zh"><div class="poem">经过漫长和激烈的战斗，神社巫女取得了胜利，妖怪被迫释放了被困在她背信弃义的诅咒中的人类村民。<br><br>那么，不是人类的话，她巢穴里的那些蜜蜂又是什么呢？</div></td></tr><tr class="tt-content" id="东方催狐谭-4" data-pos="&#91;&quot;\u4e1c\u65b9\u50ac\u72d0\u8c2d&quot;,4&#93;"><td class="tt-ja" lang="ja"><div class="poem">The secret is that she only transforms fairies for her hive.
