All of these things happening at once couldn’t be a coincidence.<br>I have to do something.”<br><br>Reimu knows the drill - very well. Something unusual happens;<br>and then there are either visitors (whether they present themselves<br>as such or not) or local residents who become strong enough to cause<br>trouble.<br><br>Although, “unusual” has been becoming a less effective word<br>to use in these incidents, as they are called, since they’ve been<br>occurring much more frequently; it’s difficult to tell what’s “usual”<br>around here anymore.<br><br>Flying into the scene comes Marisa - a totally normal magician.<br>Ever since the unusual light shows have started occurring - rainbows,<br>auroras, phenomena that probably haven’t been named yet -<br>she’s been “researching” them.<br><br>Aya, not wanting to bother the Shrine Maiden from her work any<br>longer, flies off to find another witness to interview.<br><br>Marisa: “Hey, Reimu, are you seein’ this? There’s crazy stuff happening<br>everywhere! I think my magic’s goin’ out of control a bit too-”<br><br>Reimu: “Good, you’re just who I wanted to see.”<br><br>Marisa: “Huh? Don’t tell me we’re goin’ crazy-people-shooting again<br>already. Didn’t we just give that door person the boot?”<br><br>Reimu: “Don’t you think I would like a break, too? Come on, let’s get<br>this over with already.”<br><br>And the two of them are off, each going their own way, leaving the<br>shrine grounds empty. Due to the new barrier’s influence on magic power,<br>the forest simply radiates with it. Night is approaching fast, and even so,<br>the strange light persists.<br><br>-- However, in these dark hours, many monsters and youkai awaken. <br>With this new barrier in effect, Gensokyo is in complete danger; <br>the enhanced magic power has turned the land of fantasy into a designated<br>battleground.<br><br>Somewhere, unknown to the heroines.
